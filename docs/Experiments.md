# LunarMatch — Robustness Experiments & Synthetic Benchmarks

**Category:** Scientific Evaluation & Stress-Testing

---

## 1. Experimental Methodology
To evaluate the resilience of the correspondence and registration pipeline under lunar orbital conditions, LunarMatch integrates a **Robustness Laboratory**.
The laboratory applies controlled mathematical perturbations to lunar base images and evaluates the degradation characteristics across five dimensions:

1. **Illumination Variation ($\Delta B$):**
   - Simulates multi-temporal lunar imagery captured under steep solar elevation and azimuth differences (e.g. dawn vs noon vs sunset shadows).
   - Sweep: Brightness shifts from $-50$ to $+50$ intensity levels.
2. **Scale Differential ($s$):**
   - Simulates cross-sensor resolution gaps (such as Chandrayaan-2 OHRC at ~0.25m/px vs TMC-2 at 5m/px or IIRS at 20m/px).
   - Sweep: Scale factors from $-20\%$ to $+20\%$.
3. **In-Plane Rotation ($\theta$):**
   - Simulates orbital yaw and attitude deviations between disparate satellite ground tracks.
   - Sweep: Angles from $-15^\circ$ to $+15^\circ$.
4. **Translation Offset ($T_x, T_y$):**
   - Simulates footprint georeferencing uncertainty.
   - Sweep: Sub-pixel to macro shifts ($-30\text{ px}$ to $+30\text{ px}$).

---

## 2. Mandatory Disclaimer Policy
Every result generated by the Robustness Laboratory contains the prominent scientific disclaimer:
> **CONTROLLED SYNTHETIC EXPERIMENT:**
> Evaluates algorithm degradation curves against known mathematical perturbations. Never represents natural Chandrayaan telemetry.

---

## 3. Performance Metrics Monitored
For each perturbation increment, the experiment service records:
- Inlier Count
- Inlier Ratio (%)
- Spatial Coverage (%)
- Reprojection RMSE (px)
- Latency (ms)
- Registration Reliability Status (`SUCCESSFUL`, `LOW_CONFIDENCE`, `NOT_RELIABLE`)

All experiment runs are automatically serialized and saved to:
`backend/experiments/results/{experiment_id}.json`
